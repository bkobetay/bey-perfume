# Pixel town artwork

Final game asset: `dist/assets/pixel-almaty-landmarks.png`.
Created with the built-in ImageGen tool as an edit of `pixel-almaty-town.png`. The original pixel town is kept alongside it. The town is an artistic game scene inspired by Almaty; landmark locations are adapted to its playable streets rather than an exact geographic plan.

Two recognizable buildings were added: Dostyk Plaza with its blue glass entrance and beige wings, and Hotel Kazakhstan with its slender tower and golden crown. Architecture references came from [Dostyk Plaza's official owner](https://tspgreit.com/portfolio/dostyk-plaza/) and [Hotel Kazakhstan](https://kazakhstanhotel.kz/). These photos were used only as visual references; the game uses the generated pixel illustration.
